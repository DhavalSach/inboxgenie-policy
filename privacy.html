Inbox Genie is a mobile application developed by Dhaval Sachdeva. This Privacy Policy explains how Inbox Genie collects, uses, stores, and shares information when users access the app and related services. Google requires apps using Google API Services to publish a privacy policy that explains how user data is accessed, used, stored, and shared.

1. Information Collected

Inbox Genie may collect account information such as name, username, email address, authentication-related identifiers, and connected mailbox information needed to create and maintain a user account.

If a user connects a Gmail account, Inbox Genie may access and process email-related data needed for user-facing app features, such as sender information, subject lines, labels, categories, timestamps, message identifiers, attachment indicators, and similar inbox metadata used for organization and cleanup workflows.

Inbox Genie may also collect limited technical information such as device platform, app version, session-related information, and diagnostic or error data needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

2. How Information Is Used

Inbox Genie uses collected information to authenticate users, connect authorized mailboxes, analyze inbox activity, provide sender grouping, generate cleanup suggestions, support filtering workflows, and enable user-requested actions such as archive, trash, mark as read, or related inbox-management actions.

Information may also be used to maintain service reliability, troubleshoot issues, improve app performance, and provide user support.

3. Google User Data

If a user chooses to connect Gmail, Inbox Genie accesses Google user data only with user authorization and only to provide user-facing features requested by that user. Google requires apps to disclose how Google user data is accessed, used, stored, and shared, and the published privacy policy must accurately reflect those practices.

Inbox Genie does not sell Google user data and does not use Google user data for advertising, unrelated profiling, or market research. Inbox Genie may display advertisements through advertising providers. Advertising providers may process device or advertising-related information according to their own policies; Inbox Genie does not provide Gmail message content or Google user data to advertising providers for advertising purposes.

4. Storage and Retention

Inbox Genie may store account information and synced mailbox-related data on backend systems in order to operate features such as mailbox connection, email indexing, sender grouping, analytics, and cleanup workflows. The app has been designed as a MongoDB-backed inbox management product.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain operational records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and protect system integrity. If mailbox access is removed or account deletion is requested, associated data may be deleted or de-identified subject to legal, operational, backup, fraud-prevention, and security requirements.

5. Sharing of Information

Inbox Genie does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only in limited circumstances, such as with service providers or infrastructure providers supporting the app, when required by law, to protect rights and security, or in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, or business transfer subject to applicable legal obligations.

Inbox Genie does not expose private Gmail content to other users without the affected user’s consent. Google policies restrict exposing non-public API-derived content to other users or third parties without explicit opt-in consent.

6. Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, or loss. Google’s policies also require developers handling Google user data to protect that data appropriately.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so absolute security cannot be promised.

7. User Choices

Users may choose whether to connect a Gmail account to Inbox Genie. Users may also remove Inbox Genie access through their Google account settings or by using available mailbox or account-management features in the app, where applicable. Google requires apps using Google data to explain how users can understand and manage that access.

Users may contact the developer regarding access, correction, or deletion requests related to account information, subject to legal, operational, and security retention requirements.

8. Children’s Privacy

Inbox Genie is not intended for children under 13, and the service does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If such information is discovered, reasonable steps will be taken to delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If material changes are made to how data, including Google user data, is accessed, used, stored, or shared, this policy will be updated and additional notice or consent will be provided where required. Google requires apps to keep disclosures accurate and updated when practices change.
