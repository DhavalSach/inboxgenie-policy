Delete Your Inbox Genie Data

Unlink a connected Gmail mailbox

  1. Sign in to Inbox Genie.
  2. Open Manage Accounts.
  3. Select the Gmail mailbox you want to remove.
  4. Choose the option to unlink the mailbox.
  5. Request and verify the OTP sent to your Inbox Genie account email.

When a mailbox is unlinked, Inbox Genie revokes Google access, stops syncing that mailbox, and deletes indexed mailbox data associated with the mailbox.

Request account deletion

To request deletion of your Inbox Genie account and associated account data, email support.inboxgenie@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Inbox Genie account.

Some limited information may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or backup integrity, consistent with the Privacy Policy.
